# Voice

Plain, specific, a little warm. We talk like an engineer who has read the
repo, not like a landing page.

- **Sentence case** for headings, buttons, nav, labels: "Start the survey",
  "Rules & skills", "Show my stack". Never ALL CAPS.
- **Product name** in running text: STACK IT FAST (and STACK IT FIRST). The
  lowercase `stackitfast` is the logo, not a word in sentences.
- **Numbers over adjectives**: "116 architectures, read from source", not
  "hundreds of battle-tested stacks". Numbers come from the database.
- **Say the limit**: "Reads the public repo. Saves a PNG." "About 2 minutes."
- **Buttons are verbs + object**: "Explore stacks", "Get the rules",
  "Make my card". Avoid "Learn more", "Click here", "Submit".
- **Stacky doesn't talk.** He appears; the copy stays ours. No speech bubbles
  with jokes, no "Oops!".
- **Errors** say what happened and what to do: "That repo is private. Paste a
  public one." / "GitHub is limiting requests. Try again in a minute."
- **Empty states**: one sentence and one action: "No stacks match these
  filters." [Clear filters]
- **No emoji, no exclamation marks** in UI copy.
